# Facts are mechanical, completeness is a mark

The mechanical floor, in one sentence: facts and arithmetic stay mechanical;
editorial completeness is a mark in the table. The catalog-completeness gate
broke that floor. It refused to deal a free route unless a curated catalog
entry and a resolved summary existed, so editorial work — the summaries
refresh, a research backlog by nature — became an admission verdict. A route
perfectly able to serve was excluded for missing prose, and a policy entry
was required before a free model could be dealt at all. The gate is retired.
Admission now checks only what is measured or declared — posture, Exposure,
Footprint against capacity, rostered Capability bands, the free-quota reserve,
and the material the run is given — and a missing summary is a mark in the
table plus one prose line, never an exclusion.

## Considered Options

- **Keep the catalog-completeness gate** — rejected: it makes a refresh
  state a precondition for use. Completeness is owned by the research cycle,
  which is allowed to lag; turning that lag into a refusal means the first
  run can be blocked by work the user did not know existed and cannot see in
  the deal, and the policy surface grows an entry that exists only to admit
  what the serving facts already admitted.
- **Admit silently, marking nothing** — rejected: it hides the gap. A reader
  could not tell a summary-backed route from an unsummarised one, the Gap
  would never be seen, and the refresh cycle would lose its most natural
  signal. The mark is what keeps completeness visible once it stops being
  mechanical.
- **Chosen: facts gate, completeness marks** — the admission gates keep
  their mechanical checks and a missing summary prints where the pick is
  made. The plans catalog is unaffected: it decides which plan routes exist
  at all, so an uncataloged plan id never becomes a candidate — a pool of
  facts, not an admission verdict.

## Consequences

- No policy entry is required before a free model may be dealt, and free
  routes appear from the first run.
- The trade: a free route without a summary may win a deal on facts alone,
  with no summary-backed domain guidance behind it. The mark is visible at
  the pick, so the user can choose otherwise, and the Gap stays recorded as
  refresh work rather than disappearing into an exclusion.
- The parser that validates a catalog's stated Exposure stays: it is a fact
  the Exposure machinery runs on, not a completeness check, and deleting it
  would let a catalog claim contradict the serving identity.
- Editorial completeness never silently becomes mechanical again: a new
  check must be a fact or arithmetic to gate, and anything else is a mark.
  This is where a future check belongs, decided once rather than argued per
  feature.
- Sensitive handling is untouched: a ticket shape still splits on the
  material the run is given, never on catalog state, and the held lane keeps
  its local-only rule (ADR-0008).
