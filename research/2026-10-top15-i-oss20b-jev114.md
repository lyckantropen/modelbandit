## openai/gpt-oss-20b

### Benchmark standing

- **First-party (OpenAI, 2025-08-05)**: 21B total / 3.6B active MoE (24 layers, 32 experts, 4 active),
  native 128k context, MXFP4 natively quantised, runs "on edge devices with just 16 GB of memory", and
  "matches or exceeds OpenAI o3-mini on these same evals… even outperforming it on competition
  mathematics and health" ([OpenAI release](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-oss/),
  [model card](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10925)).
- **Artificial Analysis**: the AA model page (read 2026-10-25) shows **gpt-oss-20b (High): Intelligence
  Index 9 (#50/141 in its open-weights class), 173 tok/s (#9/64 by speed), $0.05/$0.22 per M as AA
  measures provider pricing, $0.01 per Intelligence-Index task, 131k context, knowledge cutoff May 2024
  ([AA](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gpt-oss-20b)).
- **OpenRouter's AA feed row** (as_of 2026-10-24) instead reads `gpt-oss-20b (Low)`, intelligence **10**,
  coding **null**, agentic **null**, with row prices $0.024/$0.112
  ([feed](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/benchmarks?source=artificial-analysis)) — the same row is embedded
  on the model page as "gpt-oss-20b (Low) … score 10"
  ([model page](https://openrouter.ai/openai/gpt-oss-20b)). **[anchor correction: confirmed — but the
  AA-vs-OpenRouter pair is effort-mismatched]**: OpenRouter reports the *Low*-effort variant at 10,
  AA's page reports the *High*-effort variant at 9; there is **no coding or agentic index for this
  model at all**, unlike gpt-oss-120b (coding 30.4, agentic 3.7 in the same feed). Treat "10 vs 11.6
  for 120b" as roughly-halved intelligence at best, and do not read the nulls as zero.
- **Design Arena** (via OpenRouter): dataviz ELO 933 (rank 124), website ELO 852 (rank 143) — bottom
  quartile, i.e. no design/UI standing
  ([model page](https://openrouter.ai/openai/gpt-oss-20b)).
- **Independent academic eval**: "gpt-oss-20B consistently outperforms gpt-oss-120B on several
  benchmarks, such as HumanEval and MMLU… Both models demonstrate mid-tier overall performance within
  the current open source landscape, with relative strength in code generation and notable weaknesses
  in multilingual tasks" ([arXiv 2508.12461](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12461), v3 2025-12-13).
- **Catalog facts (verified 2026-10-25)**: created 2025-08-05T17:17:09Z, 131,072 ctx,
  $0.018/M in, $0.09/M out, $0.009/M cache read, text→text, reasoning mandatory with efforts
  high/medium/low (default medium), **max completion 32,768** (vs 65,536 for 120b)
  ([catalog](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models),
  [endpoints](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/openai/gpt-oss-20b/endpoints)).
  Batch route: $0.024/$0.112 with 117,964 max completion
  ([catalog](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models)).

### Opinions

- **Launch framing** was "basically Phi-5" — the top HN thread title itself, arguing gpt-oss is a
  synthetic-data small-model exercise rather than a real OpenAI-tier model
  ([HN 44828884](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44828884),
  [Sean Goedecke](https://www.seangoedecke.com/gpt-oss-is-phi-5/)). In that same 219-comment thread,
  hands-on testers were positive on the 20B specifically ("GPT-OSS picked up on it… much more thorough
  at explaining code") against Gemma 3 12B / Phi-4 / Qwen2.5-coder-14B
  ([HN 44828884](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44828884)).
- **Hugging Face users**, three days after release: "the model seems to have some issues with quality
  where it breaks while coding or performing bad at creative tasks"; a responder lists "broadly and
  profoundly ignorant outside of coding, math and STEM… aggressively aligned for safety" and concludes
  "this model is crap" — also blaming broken reasoning-token round-trips in third-party runners
  ([HF discussion #92](https://huggingface.co/openai/gpt-oss-20b/discussions/92)). Another thread leads
  with "This model is unbelievably ignorant", citing SimpleQA 6.7/100
  ([HF discussion #14](https://huggingface.co/openai/gpt-oss-20b/discussions/14)).
- **Counterweight**: a local-inference review reports "coherent, on-theme creative writing", accurate
  math/logic and good multi-step instruction following ([slash.co](https://slash.co/articles/testing-openais-latest-gpt-oss-20b-model-on-my-local-machine/)).
  A safety study finds reliability problems specific to low-resource languages (Hausa probing)
  ([arXiv 2510.01266](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01266v1)). Sentiment on the *20b* is genuinely
  split, not uniformly negative.

### Known for

The Apache-2.0, single-16GB-GPU reasoning toy — o3-mini-class claims at $0.018/$0.09 on OpenRouter and
~225 tok/s on a 4090 locally, bought mostly for local/self-hosted and cheap-tool-calling work.

### Caveats

- **Local footprint has a context cliff**: model card numbers are 12.0 GB weights + ~0.2 GB per 8,192
  tokens KV; Q4_K_M GGUF is 13.3 GB, but "at 128k context, generation collapses to ~9 tok/s regardless
  of GPU" — cap context at 8k on 16 GB cards (225 tok/s on RTX 4090 at 8k)
  ([RunAIHome 2026-06-09](https://runaihome.com/blog/gpt-oss-20b-local-ai-hardware-guide-2026/),
  [llama.cpp guide](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions/15396)).
- **Serving/routing spread (2026-10-25)**: 11 endpoints — fp8 (Darkbloom $0.018/$0.09 cheapest,
  SiliconFlow), fp4 (AkashML, CoreWeave, Parasail), bf16 (DekaLLM, DeepInfra), unknown (Bedrock ×2,
  Google, Groq); **max completion varies 8,192 (SiliconFlow) → 32,768 (Darkbloom, Google) → 117,964**,
  and Google's endpoint showed 0% uptime in the 30-minute snapshot
  ([endpoints API](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/openai/gpt-oss-20b/endpoints)). Catalog-level
  32,768 max completion is half of gpt-oss-120b's — long generations silently truncate.
- **Quality floor**: no coding/agentic index, mid-tier at best in the independent ten-benchmark study,
  weak multilingual and weak on factual recall (SimpleQA ~6.7) — see Opinions for links. Knowledge
  cutoff May 2024 ([AA](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gpt-oss-20b)); text-only, no vision
  ([catalog](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models)).
- **License**: Apache 2.0, commercial use allowed ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-oss/),
  [GitHub](https://github.com/openai/gpt-oss), [AA](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gpt-oss-20b));
  training data opaque.
- **Traffic reality — [anchor correction]**: the *rank* "#14 writing" could not be reproduced, but the
  *shares* were. On the 7-day task board (as_of 2026-10-24) gpt-oss-20b appears in exactly one tag,
  **content_writing, rank #7 of the 10 rows the API returns**, at **3.7% usage share but 0.3% token
  share** within a tag that is only 4.5% of platform usage / 2.4% of tokens
  ([classifications API](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/classifications/task?window=7d)). The API caps at
  top-10 per tag regardless of `limit`, so a #14-of-15 view exists somewhere I could not reach — say
  "#14 (writing domain)" with the caveat that the reachable board shows #7/10 with the same 3.7% share.
- **"Ranks on one board but no measurable tokens" is real, not a slug mismatch**: across the whole
  2026-09-25→2026-10-24 daily-token window, `openai/gpt-oss-20b` has **zero days** in the top-51 daily
  list, while `openai/gpt-oss-120b` — same slug pattern — is present 28/30 days at 45–108 B tokens/day
  ([rankings API](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/datasets/rankings-daily)). The 51st-place floor was
  45.6 B tokens on 2026-10-24, so gpt-oss-20b is below that every day. Derived from the tag shares
  above, its writing-tag traffic is ≈0.007% of platform tokens (≈1–2 B/day against a 23.4 T-token day)
  and ≈0.17% of platform requests — a usage/token skew of ~12×, i.e. **many very short calls**. Whether
  that is short-form drafting, cheap classification mis-tagged as writing, or a classifier artifact
  cannot be distinguished from public data; combined with the community "bad at creative tasks"
  reports, treat the writing rank as a **weak signal**, not evidence of prose quality.

## typesafe/jev-1.14-20261014

### Benchmark standing

- **Still not an LLM, and still not in the catalog.** Neither `typesafe/jev-1.14` nor `typesafe/jev-1.13`
  appears in `/api/v1/models` — only `typesafe/jev-router` does **[anchor correction: confirmed]**
  ([catalog, read 2026-10-25](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models)). How it is served is now documented:
  one provider (TypeSafe), two surfaces — `POST https://openrouter.ai/api/alpha/decisions` (Decisions
  API) and `POST https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/systemone` (TypeSafe SDK base-URL swap), both billed to
  the OpenRouter account, output modality literally `decisions`
  ([OpenRouter Jev guide](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/community/jev),
  [endpoints API](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/typesafe/jev-1.14-20261014/endpoints)).
- **Price/latency (2026-10-25)**: $0.042/M input, **$0 output** — identical to 1.13 — with advertised
  context 262,144, max completion 235,929, single endpoint, P50 0.13 s, 100% uptime and 99.96%
  availability over 3 days
  ([endpoints API](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/typesafe/jev-1.14-20261014/endpoints),
  [model page](https://openrouter.ai/typesafe/jev-1.14),
  [provider page](https://openrouter.ai/provider/typesafe)).
  **[anchor correction on context]**: TypeSafe's own docs list **64k per-request / 32k per-question by
  default**, and 262k only with the `TypeSafe-Danger-Zone-Long-Context: true` header; OpenRouter
  advertises the 262K number and its FAQ says requests over ~32 KB of text or *any* image
  automatically run in long-context mode, which TypeSafe calls "slower and less accurate"
  ([TypeSafe docs](https://docs.typesafe.ai/models),
  [OpenRouter guide](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/community/jev)).
- **Vendor benchmark** (they state they "dislike public benchmarks"): one internal chart, 1.14.0 = 60.0
  vs 1.13.0 = 58.9 overall intelligence, open-weight rivals 33.8–46.7, banner "Warning: AI benchmarks.
  Please don't trust them", only open weights included, and "likely biased towards our models"
  ([TypeSafe blog 2026-10-14](https://typesafe.ai/blog/jev-1-14),
  [explainx fact-check](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/jev-1-14-danger-zone-multimodal-box-long-context-claimed-vs-verified-2026)).
- **Independent, and it does not flatter 1.14**: JevBench (independent, 1,500 decisions each, draw
  v1.7, read 2026-10-25) shows **Jev 1.14.0 as an unranked reference: Intelligence 59.9, Calibration
  90.1, Capability 75.0, $0.032/1k decisions, 0.23 s — against Jev 1.13.0's 63.6 / 90.6 / 77.1** at the
  same $0.032/1k. Ten open-weight Gemma-4-31B-derived "Jev-class" reproductions now score 77.6–84.3,
  above both ([JevBench](https://benchmarkheaven.com/jev-models)). Read as one reference row, not a
  verdict: TypeSafe's own chart has 1.14 *ahead* of 1.13 — sources disagree.
- **The predecessor's headline weakness is verified, and scoped to 1.13**: Vals AI's independent eval
  (400 SEC claim-verification items + a preregistered 12-subtask LegalBench slice, 12 systems, Jev
  **1.13.0**) found Jev ties frontier models on claim verification (390/400, $0.02/1k vs GPT-6 Astra's
  1/498th the cost) but is **last of twelve on LegalBench** raw and class-balanced accuracy (0.730 vs
  0.932 for the leader), answering "Yes" to all 33 items on one contract-NLI subtask, and its
  calibration did **not** transfer: ECE 0.011 (best of twelve) on claim verification but **0.108
  (worst)** on LegalBench. At a 1% error budget it automated 95% at 1.6% error — over budget
  ([Vals AI](https://www.vals.ai/blogs/independent-evaluation-of-jev)). **No independent evaluation of
  1.14 itself was found** beyond JevBench's reference row.

### Opinions

- **Launch heat**: TypeSafe came out of two years of stealth on 2026-09-15 claiming 193.6× faster /
  444.6× cheaper than frontier LLMs and "zero hallucinations"; the HN launch thread hit ~1,500 points,
  and the independent audit concludes the multiples "depend entirely on what you compare it to — the
  first independent tests measured about 5×", that "can't hallucinate" is "true of shape, not of truth",
  that the calibration claim (RLCD) has "no paper, no calibration curve, no ablation", and that TypeSafe's
  CEO agreed "exactly right" to the description "basically a zero-shot classifier"
  ([The Jev File, 2026-09-16](https://jev.novcog.us.com/),
  [HN 49717558](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49717558)). Calibration *is* externally measured
  now — JevBench's 90.1–90.6 and Vals' split ECE above — so the "unverified" charge is partly dated.
- **What 1.14 actually changed (2026-10-14)**: "small, subtle improvements to everything", largest
  expected gain in **multilingual** (no numbers published; docs still say English is where accuracy is
  best); new **`box`** bounding-box type, **1.14-only**; **images** and **262k long context** are
  header-gated "danger zone" features that TypeSafe explicitly says have "way too much jaggedness for us
  to vouch for for production" — and images work on 1.13 too, so they are not a 1.14 reason to upgrade
  ([TypeSafe blog](https://typesafe.ai/blog/jev-1-14),
  [TypeSafe docs](https://docs.typesafe.ai/models),
  [explainx](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/jev-1-14-danger-zone-multimodal-box-long-context-claimed-vs-verified-2026)).
  TypeSafe also added an org-level opt-in letting it read your inputs/outputs to hunt jaggedness (no
  training) ([same](https://www.explainx.ai/blog/jev-1-14-danger-zone-multimodal-box-long-context-claimed-vs-verified-2026)).
- **Availability elsewhere**: Vercel AI Gateway added Jev 1.14 on the same typed-Choice/Score/Boolean
  shape ([Vercel changelog](https://vercel.com/changelog/typesafe-ais-jev-1-14-is-now-available-on-ai-gateway));
  OpenRouter's guide notes 22 decision models from 13 makers now share the Decisions API (OpenAI,
  Microsoft, Perplexity, Cloudflare, Inception, Liquid, Upstage…), so Jev is no longer the only option
  and "probabilities differ between models… pick thresholds again"
  ([OpenRouter guide](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/community/jev)).

### Known for

A typed, calibrated decision primitive — parallel-sampled Choice/Noul/Score answers with probabilities,
~0.13 s and free output — that routes, classifies, verifies and gates agent actions instead of writing text.

### Caveats

- **Traffic: migration has started but 1.13 still dominates [verified]**. From the daily rankings
  (2026-09-25→2026-10-24): 1.13 = 13.33 T tokens over 30 days; 1.14 = **2.02 T over its first 10 days
  (2026-10-15→10-24)**, i.e. 141 B/day at launch rising to 189–374 B/day, while 1.13 stayed flat over
  the same window (4.18 T in its last 10 days vs 4.29 T in its first 10) — 1.14 is **additive so far,
  not a switch**. Platform share moved 2026-10-15: 1.13 1.83% + 1.14 0.58% = 2.41% → 2026-10-24: 1.58%
  + 0.81% = 2.39% — **[anchor correction: the combined share is flat, only the mix moved]**
  ([rankings API](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/datasets/rankings-daily)).
- **Task-board standing (7d, as_of 2026-10-24)**: 1.14 is #2 in classification_tagging (9.3% usage /
  9.5% token), #4 finance_trading (8.0%), #7 qa_knowledge (3.8%), #8 translation (3.6%),
  #9 code:review_security (2.4%), #10 data:extraction (2.9%) and #10 security_audit (1.3%) — all
  verified; **1.13 still leads 1.13-relevant tags**: #1 classification_tagging (22.8%),
  #1 code:review_security (18.4%), #2 finance_trading (12.8%), #2 qa_knowledge (9.6%), #2 math (7.2%)
  ([classifications API](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/classifications/task?window=7d)).
  The domain-level ranks in the brief (#8 research / #15 coding / #14 ops, and #14 writing for
  gpt-oss-20b) are **not reproducible from the reachable endpoints** (that API returns 10 rows/tag and
  has no research/coding/ops groups) — the *shares* all match exactly, so treat the ranks as
  **[unverified: likely a deeper view of the same board]**.
  **[anchor correction on the predecessor's share]**: the inherited "29.7% of classification traffic"
  is now **22.8% for 1.13 alone, 32.1% for 1.13+1.14 combined**.
- **Version pinning works, and matters**: versioned slugs resolve independently on OpenRouter
  (`typesafe/jev-1.14-20261014` and `typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917` both return their own endpoint
  records) ([endpoints API](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/typesafe/jev-1.14-20261014/endpoints),
  [1.13 endpoints](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/typesafe/jev-1.13-20260917/endpoints)); TypeSafe
  commits to **60 days' warning before 1.13.0 is deprecated, 30 days for 1.14.0**, documents that
  `jev-latest`/`jev-preview` aliases move out from under you, and explicitly advises sending the
  versioned ID once you have tuned confidence thresholds against a version
  ([blog](https://typesafe.ai/blog/jev-1-14), [docs](https://docs.typesafe.ai/models)).
- **Jaggedness to design around**: accuracy shifts as state grows ("Jev 1.13 jaggedness" doc), long
  context and images are slower and less accurate by TypeSafe's own wording, Box has **no "not found"
  answer** (it always returns four edges — pair it with a Noul), multimodality degrades everything,
  and non-English is explicitly "not equally well" supported
  ([OpenRouter guide](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/community/jev),
  [TypeSafe docs](https://docs.typesafe.ai/models),
  [blog](https://typesafe.ai/blog/jev-1-14)).
- **Still never generation**: no prose, no reasoning traces, no explanations, "not a drop-in
  replacement for a chat model"; Box/images extend the *typed* surface only, and the images/Box/long-context
  trio is advertised by TypeSafe as not production-vouched
  ([OpenRouter guide](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/community/jev),
  [blog](https://typesafe.ai/blog/jev-1-14)).
- **Licensing/terms**: closed weights, no paper; TypeSafe Terms of Use apply and OpenRouter's data
  policy sits on top ([provider page](https://openrouter.ai/provider/typesafe),
  [terms](https://typesafe.ai/legal/terms)). "Jev is not trained on customer requests or responses";
  zero data retention is an **enterprise** arrangement, and a third-party audit notes the policy sets no
  general retention period ([TypeSafe docs](https://docs.typesafe.ai/models),
  [piiblock](https://www.piiblock.com/blog/typesafe-jev-data-privacy)). Not EU in-region routable
  ([model page](https://openrouter.ai/typesafe/jev-1.14)).
- **Price could be subsidised**: TypeSafe itself says it "cannot prove the price is not subsidised"
  ([The Jev File](https://jev.novcog.us.com/)) — relevant if you build on $0.042/M-in/$0-out.

## Policy summaries

**openai/gpt-oss-20b** — The Apache-2.0, 16 GB-GPU sibling of gpt-oss-120b (21B/3.6B active, 131k ctx,
$0.018/$0.09 on OpenRouter as of 2026-10-25) for local, privacy-bound or cheap tool-calling work, but
AA puts it at intelligence 10 measured at *Low* effort (coding and agentic indexes both null) against
120b's 11.6 High, and it is bottom-quartile on Design Arena. It shows a #7-of-10 content-writing rank
with 3.7% usage but only 0.3% token share (7-day board, as of 2026-10-24) while never once entering the
daily top-51 token rankings in the 30 days to 2026-10-24 — many tiny calls, not real writing volume, so
do not read the writing rank as prose quality (community reports call creative output weak and
"ignorant outside coding/math"). Pick it for local inference and near-free short structured calls; mind
the 32,768 completion cap (8,192 on SiliconFlow), the 11-endpoint quant zoo, the 128k-context local
speed collapse, and May-2024 knowledge.

**typesafe/jev-1.14-20261014** — Not an LLM: a typed, calibrated decision primitive (Choice/Noul/Score
plus a new 1.14-only Box type) served through OpenRouter's Decisions API at $0.042/M input with free
output, P50 0.13 s, single provider — absent from `/api/v1/models` but pinnable by versioned slug, with
TypeSafe promising 60 days' notice before 1.13 is deprecated. It holds #2 of classification_tagging at
9.3% (1.13 still leads at 22.8%) and 2.02 T tokens over its first 10 days to 2026-10-24, but that is
additive growth — 1.13 still moved 13.33 T over 30 days — and independent evidence is thin and mixed:
JevBench's reference row has 1.14 *below* 1.13 (intelligence 59.9 vs 63.6, calibration 90.1 vs 90.6),
the only vendor number is TypeSafe's own self-described-biased chart (60.0 vs 58.9), and the
"last of 12 on LegalBench / ECE 0.108 worst-in-class" result is Vals AI's measurement of **1.13**, not
1.14. Use it for classification/routing/verification/guardrail steps only — never generation or
multi-hop reasoning — design thresholds to its documented non-transfer of calibration across tasks,
re-tune thresholds after any version pin, and treat images/262k context as opt-in "danger zone"
features TypeSafe explicitly will not vouch for in production.
